The Alpha Gap

You pay an advisor to beat the market.

Two percentage points of alpha, compounded over a career, is the difference between retiring comfortable and retiring rich. Or between retiring and not.

Most investors never measure it. They look at their returns in isolation and feel fine. The benchmark would have made them more.
